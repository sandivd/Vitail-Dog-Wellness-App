## Purpose and document control

This page records consequential product, scope, design and delivery decisions separately from requirements and meeting minutes. It responds to the supervisor feedback recorded in [Standup meeting 7](https://poodles.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/poodles/pages/53608449): maintain a separate decision log and a concise requirements table containing story ID, user story, priority and acceptance criteria.

This reconstruction distinguishes explicit client responses, documented team directions and unresolved proposals. “Recorded” does not mean implemented or tested. Dates below are meeting dates where available; page-update dates are labelled. Client responses appended to Meeting 5 are not assumed to have been received on the scheduled meeting date.

**Proposed maintenance:** the current Scrum Master maintains this page; the relevant feature owner links implementation and test evidence; the client confirms business rules. These responsibilities are recommendations, not retrospective assignments. Preserve decision IDs, record amendments as new entries, and identify any entry they supersede.

## Evidence and change procedure

For each new decision: record the actual decision date, question, alternatives genuinely considered, selected option, rationale, decision-maker, status, source link and affected story IDs. Do not reconstruct votes, approvals or alternative evaluations that were not recorded. Attach implementation and test evidence later without changing a business decision's original date.

For changed decisions: add a new ID, identify the superseded entry, update dependent acceptance criteria and record the client's or team's confirmation. Meeting attendance alone is not evidence of approval.
